05 / Our Story

About Archana Farm

Rooted in hard work, humility, and a deep reverence for nature, Archana Farm is the lifelong vision of Shri Ramaswamy and his family. Rising from humble beginnings through sheer perseverance, Shri Ramaswamy completed his graduation and served for eleven years in the government sector, where he became a trusted name among thousands of sericulture farmers across more than a hundred villages. Guided by the spirit of "Marali Mannige" (returning to the soil), he left behind a permanent government post to return to his ancestral roots in farming, establishing a successful silkworm seed production center alongside the well-known Ramaswamy Military Hotel, which serves hundreds of guests daily. Beyond agriculture and hospitality, Shri Ramaswamy has dedicated his life to social welfare and Kannada literature—enrolling thousands of members into the Kannada Sahitya Parishat in Bannur Hobli and leading the community restoration of the historic Mattitaleshwara Temple. Together with his tireless wife, Smt. Manjula, their daughter Smt. Ashwini, and their son Aravind—a Mechanical Engineering graduate and M.Tech holder in Automobile Engineering working as a Senior Manager—the family embodies the true spirit of Kayaka (dedicated work), providing direct employment to over thirty people and supporting countless families.

Created in loving memory of their beloved daughter Archana, whom they lost at a young age, Archana Farm stands today as a serene agricultural haven visited by hundreds of guests. Built with love and care, it continues to grow as a premier agri-tourism destination where visitors can experience authentic countryside living, warm hospitality, and the timeless beauty of the land.
